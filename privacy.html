Privacy policy

Dan Contacts Sync is a private, personal-use application running locally on its owner's Mac.

Data and purpose

The application reads iCloud and personal Google contact data to match address-book entries, preserve existing information, and mirror approved iCloud changes into Google Contacts. Contact information can include names, email addresses, telephone numbers, postal addresses, organizations, birthdays, notes and other contact fields. Google email identity is used only to verify the intended Google account.

Transfer and storage

Contact data is transferred over HTTPS only between the owner's Mac and Apple's contact service or Google's official contact API for the requested synchronization. The application has no analytics or advertising and does not send contact data to an external synchronization vendor or an AI service. Authentication tokens and the Apple app-specific password are stored in macOS Keychain. Local contact backups, mappings and write journals are retained on the owner's Mac until the owner removes them.

Control

The owner can stop the scheduled job, revoke the Google authorization in Google Account settings, revoke the Apple app-specific password, and remove local backups. Missing source data never causes automatic contact deletion. Ambiguous matches or unsupported fields are held for review rather than silently discarded.

Limited use

Use and transfer of Google API data adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the contact synchronization described here and is not sold, used for advertising, or used for eligibility decisions.

This notice covers the local sync software. It does not change the terms of Apple, Google, or any separate assistance service used by the owner.
