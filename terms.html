Personal-use terms

Dan Contacts Sync is a private utility for its owner's accounts. It is not offered as a public hosted service and has no subscription or usage charges from the application.

The owner controls account authorization, local scheduling, backups and any contact changes. Apple and Google accounts remain subject to their respective provider terms and availability.

Synchronization must be checked against backups before relying on it. The software may hold uncertain matches and unsupported fields for manual review. No guarantee is made that every provider field can be synchronized.

Access to this informational website does not grant anyone access to the owner's contacts or credentials.
