Dan Contacts Sync

A private, free desktop tool that keeps its owner's personal Google Contacts up to date from iCloud Contacts.

iCloud is the primary address book. The scheduled process is designed to run locally on the owner's Mac, reads iCloud contacts, and adds or updates corresponding personal Google contacts. It does not synchronize email or calendar data and does not propagate contact deletions.

The tool saves local backups and holds uncertain matches for review. It is for the owner's personal use and is not a public sign-up service.

There is no subscription, advertising or paid synchronization provider.
